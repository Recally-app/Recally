# Recally Brand Colors

Recally’s color palette is designed to feel calm, focused, and developer-friendly — with a light background, strong contrast color, and a single vibrant accent.
The below is really not strict. Experiment away and see what works, but try to document or standardize experiments here or somewhere else


| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|------|--------|-----|--------|
| **Background** | Light blue | `#D8EDFD` | App and popup backgrounds |
| **Contrast / Primary Text** | Deep navy | `#26405E` | Buttons, headers, key text |
| **Accent** | Bright sky blue | `#4CA1F7` | Links, highlights, active states |

### Quick Reference
- Background → `#D8EDFD`
- Contrast → `#26405E`
- Accent → `#4CA1F7`

These colors provide a clean base for future UI theming and Chrome extension styling.
